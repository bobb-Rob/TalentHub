## 💡 Tips for Success

1. **Start meetings on time** - Respect everyone's schedule
2. **Keep standups brief** - 5 minutes max
3. **Remove blockers quickly** - Don't let issues fester
4. **Celebrate wins** - Acknowledge good work
5. **Escalate early** - Don't wait until Friday to report problems
6. **Document decisions** - Keep paper trail for learning
7. **Review code thoughtfully** - Approve on time but ensure quality

---
